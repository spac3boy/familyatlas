No internet research was conducted for A7. This audit relies on the source IDs and evidence descriptions already preserved in the owner packets.

## Conventions

- Only `verified`, `probable`, and `unresolved` are confidence states.
- A proposed ID is a future normalization recommendation, not application data.
- IDs omit uncertain name parts. A year or a neutral disambiguator is included only where needed to prevent a known collision.
- A relationship path is no stronger than its weakest link. When a parent-child bridge is `probable`, every earlier path depending on it is also `probable`.
- A named person found only in a source-discovery lead is retained as `unresolved`; the name is not enough to attach that person to the family.
- Source IDs beginning `SRC-EDMOND-…` are defined in [edmond-p-buquet.md](../people/edmond-p-buquet.md); `SRC-VERNA-…` IDs are defined in [verna-arlene-bakke-buquet.md](../people/verna-arlene-bakke-buquet.md); `SRC-A1-…` IDs are defined in [00-immediate-family.md](../people/00-immediate-family.md).
