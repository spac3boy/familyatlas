## Biographical narrative

Michael identifies Allen Comeaux of Lafayette Parish as his maternal grandfather and expressed doubt that Allen had a middle name. The source research strongly, but not conclusively, matches that family identity to Allen Paul Comeaux Sr., born in Louisiana on 27 May 1925 and associated with Carencro at the end of his life. A census-linked profile places the 1925-born Allen in the 1930 Ward One, Lafayette Parish family of Jules Comeaux and Joesette R. Comeaux, with Alton and Lena in the sibling group.

Family information and a compiled tree identify Rita LeBlanc Comeaux as Allen's spouse. Rita's obituary supplies the relevant descendant group: Russell, Allen Paul Jr., Peggy, Priscilla, and Paulette, as well as eleven grandchildren including Michael. Because the obituary directly establishes Rita's relationships but does not name Allen as the children's father, the Allen parent-child claims remain probable pending a marriage record, birth/baptism records, or another direct family record.

Allen died in June 1985; 1 June is a probable exact date from one derivative index. His occupation, service history, death place, burial place, and complete residence timeline were not established. The defensible ancestral chain currently stops at his parents, Jules and Joesette. A direct Acadian Comeaux descent may be historically plausible from surname and regional context, but the chats did not document the intervening genealogy and therefore did not attach one.

## Conflicts and discrepancies

1. **Middle name and suffix.** Michael believed Allen probably had no middle name. Derivative/compiled results use `Allen Paul Comeaux`, and Rita's obituary names a son `Allen Paul Comeaux Jr.` The converging pattern favors `Allen Paul Comeaux Sr.`, but an original birth or baptism record was not viewed. The family recollection and shorter `Allen Comeaux` form remain preserved.
2. **Death-date precision.** Multiple derivative results support June 1985. Only one located index supplied 1 June 1985. The month/year is verified; the exact day remains probable.
3. **Joesette's given name and birth date.** The census-linked profile uses `Joesette R.` and estimates birth around 1892. Searches also considered `Josette` and `Josephine`. A cemetery listing for `Mrs. Jules Comeaux` reports 29 April 1890–17 May 1977. That cemetery entry is probably Allen's mother, but it does not name her directly; the circa-1892 and 1890 values are both retained.
4. **Joesette's birth surname.** No maiden/birth surname was established. The `R.` is an initial, not evidence of a particular surname. No expanded name is inferred.
5. **Alton versus Elton.** FamilySearch identifies Allen's older brother as Alton Comeaux (1910–1978). An Ancestry result for Elton Comeaux, spouse Edmonia Benoit, contains hidden-parent birth years compatible with Jules and Joesette. The two may be the same man, but the equivalence was not proved. A different Elton born in 1916 was rejected because Lena was also born in July 1916 and the family profile identifies Alton as born in 1910.
6. **Jules's parents.** One Ancestry result suggested Joseph O. Comeaux and Marie Alfeda Cormier, but the result appeared mixed with or attached to another Jules/Jules Omer Comeaux. The source tasks did not accept that pair. Jules's parents remain unresolved.
7. **Residence versus death place.** Carencro is supported as Allen's adult/last-residence association. No source reviewed established that he died in Carencro; the death place remains unknown.
8. **Lafayette Parish versus Carencro wording.** Michael supplied Lafayette Parish, while derivative evidence narrows the association to Ward One and Carencro. These are compatible geographic levels, not competing birth-place claims.

## Rejected hypotheses and false leads

### Allen Bernard Comeaux (1921–2022)

This man was rejected as Michael's grandfather. His obituary identifies parents Edward Comeaux and Elisa Langlinais, spouse Cleida Monty, and WWII Army service in the China–Burma–India Theater. His dates and family differ from the 1925–1985 Allen in the Jules/Joesette household. None of his military or biographical facts may be assigned to `person-allen-comeaux-1925`.

### Allen Joseph Comeaux (1935–2020)

This Delcambre man was rejected. His obituary identifies parents Rene Comeaux and Elleda Blanchard, spouse Barbara Naquin, and a career as a fisherman/boat captain. His birth year, residence, parents, spouse, and lifespan do not match Michael's grandfather. His occupation must not be transferred.

### Julien Comeaux born 17 July 1888

This candidate was considered because his birth year resembles Jules Comeaux's. He is a different person: the FamilySearch result identifies parents Jules Onezime Comeaux and Marie Doralise Veronie, spouse Coralie Mathieu, and a fourteen-child family. He is not substituted for Allen's father.

### Joseph O. Comeaux and Marie Alfeda Cormier as Jules's parents

This was a tempting compiled-tree result, not an accepted ancestral link. The displayed result appeared to conflate or attach information from a different Jules/Jules Omer Comeaux. Without an original marriage, baptism, census, or death record tying these parents to the Jules who married Joesette, the hypothesis remains unattached.

### Alton/Elton candidates

An Elton Comeaux married to Edmonia Benoit may be Allen's brother Alton, but matching approximate parent birth years are insufficient. Another Elton born in 1916 is incompatible with the working sibling group and was rejected. The packet retains Alton Comeaux (1910–1978) as the probable brother without attaching either Elton identity.

### Surname history as lineage proof

Comeaux is a Louisiana form associated historically with Comeau, and many Louisiana Comeaux families descend from Acadian refugees. An 1803 Carencro-area source also shows the surname in the region. Neither fact establishes Allen's particular descent. The documented working chain stops at Jules and Joesette.
